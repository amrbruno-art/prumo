# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity, experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- being respectful of differing viewpoints;
- accepting constructive criticism;
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- sexualized language or imagery;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others’ private information;
- copying third-party trademarks or assets into the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and may ban contributors for behaviors they deem inappropriate.

Reports: open a private contact with the repository owner on GitHub.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
